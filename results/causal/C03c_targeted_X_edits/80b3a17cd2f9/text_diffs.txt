In a large bowl, mix together the crabmeat. Flour, minced green onion, minced ginger, soy sauce, sake or sherry, sesame oil, salt and pepper. 2. Cover and steam for about 10 minutes until the wrappers are cooked through and the filling is hot. 5. In a small bowl, mix all dipping sauce ingredients and whisk to blend. 6. Arrange hot dumplings on a plate and sprinkle with green onion tops. Serve immediately with the dipping sauce.

BEFORE: Flemish Beef Roast (Or Stew) with Vegetables Recipe  Ingredients 1 2-3 pound beef roast, trimmed 2 tablespoons vegetable oil 1 large onion, chopped 2 carrots, chopped - 2 celery stalks chopped 3 cloves garlic, minced 1 teaspoon dried thyme 1 teaspoon dried rosemary 2 tablespoons tomato paste 1 cup beef broth 1 cup red wine 2 cups chopped mushrooms Salt and pepper to taste  Instructions 1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. 2. Heat vegetable oil in a large Dutch oven or pot over medium high heat. Add beef roast and brown on all sides about 8 minutes per side. Remove and set aside. 3. Add chopped onion, carrots, celery, garlic, thyme and rosemary to pot. Cook for about 5 minutes stirring occasionally until vegetables are softened. 4. Stir in tomato paste, beef broth, red wine and mushrooms. Bring to a simmer. 5. Season beef roast with salt and pepper. Place roast back in pot, spooning vegetable mixture on top. 6. Cover pot with a lid and transfer to preheated oven. Roast for about 1 hour and 15 minutes or until beef is tender and vegetables are cooked through. 7. Remove roast from pot and let rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving. Serve with vegetable mixture on the side. Enjoy.

BEFORE: Asparagus with Prosciutto di Parma Pecorino Pepato and Poached Eggs  Ingredients  -  1 bunch asparagus (about 1 pound) 6 large eggs 4 tablespoons unsalted butter 4 thin slices prosciutto di Parma torn into pieces 1/2 cup grated Pecorino Pepato cheese 1/2 cup heavy cream 1/2 teaspoon white pepper 1/4 teaspoon salt  Instructions 1. Bring a pot of water to a boil over high heat. Meanwhile, fill a large bowl with ice water and set aside. Cut the tips off the asparagus and set aside. Cut the stalks into 1-inch pieces and set aside. Blanch the tips in the boiling water for 1 minute. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the tips to the ice water and chill for 1 minute. Drain, pat dry, and set aside. Blanch the stalks for 4 to 6 minutes or until tender. Drain, pat dry, and set aside. 2. Crack the eggs into individual ramekins or cups. Fill a large skillet with water and add the white vinegar. Bring the water to a simmer over medium heat. Carefully slide the eggs into the water and cook for 3 to 5 minutes or until the whites are set and the yolks are cooked as desired. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the eggs to a towel to drain. 3. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the prosciutto and cook for 2 to 3 minutes or until the ham is crisp. Add the asparagus and cook for 2 to 3 minutes or until tender-crisp. Add the cheese and cook for 1 minute or until melted. Stir in the heavy cream, pepper and salt and cook for 1 minute or until heated through. 4. To serve, divide the asparagus and prosciutto among individual plates. Top each with a poached egg and some of the cheese sauce.  Nutrition Facts Calories, 383 Fat, 29g Cholesterol, 443mg Sodium, 1152mg Carbs, 6g Fiber, 1g Sugars, 3g Protein, 23g

BEFORE: Delightful Lemon Cake Recipe  Ingredients  2 cups all purpose flour  1 ½ teaspoons baking powder, ¼ teaspoon salt -3 large eggs, room temperature  1 cup granulated sugar  2 tablespoons lemon zest, and ¾ cup fresh lemon juice  1 cup buttermilk, room temperature, ½ cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled  2 teaspoons vanilla extract  For the glaze  1 cup powdered sugar -2-3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice  Instructions, 1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and prepare a 9x13 inch baking pan by greasing it with cooking spray or butter and coating it with flour.  2. In a medium sized bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.  3. In a separate large bowl, beat the eggs and sugar until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes using an electric mixer. Add in the lemon zest and juice and continue mixing until well incorporated.  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet mixture while alternating with the buttermilk, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.  5. Stir in the melted and cooled butter and vanilla extract until fully mixed.  6. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and smooth out the top using a spatula. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.  7. Allow the cake to cool completely before making the glaze. To make the glaze. Whisk together the powdered sugar and lemon juice in a small bowl until smooth. Drizzle over the cooled cake and let it set for several hours before serving. Enjoy.

BEFORE: Asparagus with Prosciutto di Parma Pecorino Pepato and Poached Eggs  Ingredients  -  1 bunch asparagus (about 1 pound) 6 large eggs 4 tablespoons unsalted butter 4 thin slices prosciutto di Parma torn into pieces 1/2 cup grated Pecorino Pepato cheese 1/2 cup heavy cream 1/2 teaspoon white pepper 1/4 teaspoon salt  Instructions 1. Bring a pot of water to a boil over high heat. Meanwhile, fill a large bowl with ice water and set aside. Cut the tips off the asparagus and set aside. Cut the stalks into 1-inch pieces and set aside. Blanch the tips in the boiling water for 1 minute. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the tips to the ice water and chill for 1 minute. Drain, pat dry, and set aside. Blanch the stalks for 4 to 6 minutes or until tender. Drain, pat dry, and set aside. 2. Crack the eggs into individual ramekins or cups. Fill a large skillet with water and add the white vinegar. Bring the water to a simmer over medium heat. Carefully slide the eggs into the water and cook for 3 to 5 minutes or until the whites are set and the yolks are cooked as desired. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the eggs to a towel to drain. 3. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the prosciutto and cook for 2 to 3 minutes or until the ham is crisp. Add the asparagus and cook for 2 to 3 minutes or until tender-crisp. Add the cheese and cook for 1 minute or until melted. Stir in the heavy cream, pepper and salt and cook for 1 minute or until heated through. 4. To serve, divide the asparagus and prosciutto among individual plates. Top each with a poached egg and some of the cheese sauce.  Nutrition Facts Calories, 383 Fat, 29g Cholesterol, 443mg Sodium, 1152mg Carbs, 6g Fiber, 1g Sugars, 3g Protein, 23g

BEFORE: Ingredients 1 cup unsalted butter, softened 1 cup all purpose flour 1 teaspoon baking powder 1/2 teaspoon baking soda 1/4 teaspoon salt 2 cups granulated sugar 1 cup (8 oz) jam (any flavor)  For the Dough 1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a small bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt. Set aside. 3. In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar on medium high speed until light and fluffy, scraping the sides of the bowl as needed. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract until combined. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined. 4. For the Filling 5. Divide the dough into two equal parts. Roll each portion out on a lightly floured surface to a thickness of 1/4 inch (6 mm). Cut into rounds with a 2-inch (5-cm) cookie cutter. Place half the rounds on the prepared baking sheet. 6. Top each round with 1 teaspoon of jam. Place another round on top of the jam, pressing the edges gently to seal. 7. Bake for 15 minutes or until the edges are lightly browned. Allow to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely. BEFORE: Delightful Lemon Cake Recipe  Ingredients  2 cups all purpose flour  1 ½ teaspoons baking powder, ¼ teaspoon salt -3 large eggs, room temperature  1 cup granulated sugar  2 tablespoons lemon zest, and ¾ cup fresh lemon juice  1 cup buttermilk, room temperature, ½ cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled  2 teaspoons vanilla extract  For the glaze  1 cup powdered sugar -2-3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice  Instructions, 1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and prepare a 9x13 inch baking pan by greasing it with cooking spray or butter and coating it with flour.  2. In a medium sized bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.  3. In a separate large bowl, beat the eggs and sugar until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes using an electric mixer. Add in the lemon zest and juice and continue mixing until well incorporated.  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet mixture while alternating with the buttermilk, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.  5. Stir in the melted and cooled butter and vanilla extract until fully mixed.  6. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and smooth out the top using a spatula. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.  7. Allow the cake to cool completely before making the glaze. To make the glaze. Whisk together the powdered sugar and lemon juice in a small bowl until smooth. Drizzle over the cooled cake and let it set for several hours before serving. Enjoy.

BEFORE: Ingredients, 1 head of broccoli, cut into small florets and 3 cloves of garlic, minced and 1/2 cup of ricotta cheese, 1/4 cup of grated parmesan cheese and 1/4 cup of hot honey 8 slices of bread and 2 tablespoons of olive oil  Instructions 1. Preheat your oven to 400°F.  2. In a large bowl, mix together the broccoli florets, minced garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt and pepper.  3. Spread the broccoli mixture onto a baking sheet and roast in the oven for 20-25 minutes. Until the broccoli is tender and slightly browned.  4. While the broccoli is roasting. Prepare the ricotta mixture. In a small bowl, mix together the ricotta cheese, parmesan cheese and a pinch of salt and pepper.  5. Once the broccoli is done. Remove it from the oven and reduce the temperature to 375°F.  6. Toast the bread slices in the oven for 5-7 minutes until lightly golden.  7. Remove the bread from the oven and spread a thin layer of ricotta mixture onto each slice.  8. Top each slice of bread with a generous spoonful of roasted broccoli and a drizzle of hot honey.  9. Serve immediately and enjoy your delicious Broccoli and Garlic Ricotta Toasts with Hot Honey.

BEFORE: Coconut Fudge Brownies Recipe  Ingredients 1 cup of granulated sugar, ½ cup of softened butter, ¼ cup of melted coconut oil and 2 large eggs 1 teaspoon vanilla extract 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour ¾ cup of unsweetened cocoa powder 2 teaspoons baking powder ½ teaspoon salt 1 cup of semisweet chocolate chips ½ cup of shredded coconut  Instructions which are 1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line an 8x8 inch baking pan with parchment paper.  2. In a large mixing bowl, cream together the sugar, butter and coconut oil until light and fluffy. Add in the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in the vanilla extract.  3. In another medium sized bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt. Gradually add this dry mixture into the wet ingredients, stirring until just combined.  4. Fold in the chocolate chips and shredded coconut until evenly distributed.  5. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and smooth out the top using a spatula or the back of a spoon. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.  6. Allow the brownies to cool completely before cutting into squares and serving. Enjoy.
