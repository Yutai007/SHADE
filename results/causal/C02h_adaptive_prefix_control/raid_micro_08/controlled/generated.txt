The Extended LTLvis Motion Planning Interface (Extended Technical Report) introduces an enhancement to the LTLvis motion planning tool, which is a popular software for robotic motion control. This update includes new functionalities like non-linear motion planning, collision detection, and path optimization. The paper offers a comprehensive overview of the extended interface, detailing its architecture, algorithms, and implementation. It also showcases a variety of case studies that highlight the interface's effectiveness in motion planning tasks. In summary, this report is an essential resource for those interested in robotics and motion planning.
