Many preppers are familiar with lifeboats, but how much supplies should one have? It's crucial to have a preparedness plan and stockpile essential items like food, water, medicine, and hygiene products. In my experience, having at least three months' worth of supplies is a good starting point. However, don't stop there! Keep adding to your stash and learning new skills to stay prepared for any situation. Remember, knowledge is power - stay informed and safe!
