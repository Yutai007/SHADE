Many preppers are familiar with lifeboats, but how much supplies should one have? It's crucial to have a preparedness plan and stockpile supplies to survive a SHTF scenario, but it can be challenging to determine what's necessary. In my experience, a minimum of three months' worth of food, water, and essential supplies like medicine and hygiene products is a good starting point. But don't be content with this; keep adding to your stockpile and learning new skills to be always prepared for any situation. Remember, knowledge is power - stay informed and stay safe!
