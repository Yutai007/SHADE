Azaleas unfurl in blush, beneath pecan trees,
Magnolias bloom, carried on a gentle breeze.
Rivers carry pollen, pine essence fills the air,
Confederate jasmine vines, sweet fragrance spreads.

Redbud trees in bloom, a clear Carolina blue,
Spanish moss hangs gently, a mystical view.
Cardinals flutter, fiery red, a passionate sight,
Camellia petals spread, a gentle deer's step.

Wisteria weaves a blanket, lilac clusters hang fair,
Buttercup field, sunlight creates a golden glare.
Mockingbirds sing, in the southern live oak's shade,
A quartet of melody and color, a southern spring.

The song of the cicadas echoes, as the day fades into evening,
Fireflies dance, in the mellow dusk-light rush.
Humid nights promise, under a silver moon's light,
A southern spring, a spellbinding charm.

The southern glow deepens, a mystical charm,
Painting the landscape with magic, a southern dream.
Life shimmers, in a languid, honeyed hue,
Spring in the South is a southern spring, a southern spring.
