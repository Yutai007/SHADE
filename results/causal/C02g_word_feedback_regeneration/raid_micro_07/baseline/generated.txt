Azaleas unfurl in blush, beneath pecan trees,
Magnolias bloom, carried on a gentle breeze.
Rivers carry pollen, pine essence fills the air,
Confederate jasmine vines, sweet fragrance spreads.

Redbud trees in bloom, a clear Carolina blue,
Spanish moss hangs gently, creating an ethereal view.
Cardinals flutter, fiery red, tender deer steps,
Camellia petals spread, wisteria weaves a blanket.

Buttercups in fields, sunlight creates amber glare,
Mockingbirds sing in southern live oak shade.
Cicadas sing, evening's hush, fireflies dance,
Humid nights, moonlit silver touch.

The south's charm, a symphony of colors and songs,
Partake in its enchantment, a southern spring's promise.
Mysterious and magical, a southern glow,
Spring in the South is a poem, forever new.
