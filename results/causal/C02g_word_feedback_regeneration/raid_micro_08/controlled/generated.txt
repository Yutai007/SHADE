The Extended LTLvis Motion Planning Interface (Extended Technical Report) introduces an enhancement to the LTLvis motion planning interface, an essential tool for motion planning in robotics. This update includes supplementary features and capabilities, such as non-linear motion planning, collision detection and avoidance, and path optimization. The report offers an in-depth explanation of the extended interface, detailing its architecture, algorithms, and implementation. Additionally, the paper presents several case studies that showcase the interface's effectiveness in various motion planning tasks. In summary, this paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field of robotics and motion planning.
