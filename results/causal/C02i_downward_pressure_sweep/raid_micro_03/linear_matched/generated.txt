Many preppers are familiar with lifeboats, but how much supplies should one have? It's crucial to have a preparedness plan and stockpile supplies to survive a SHTF scenario, but figuring out what to include can be daunting. In my experience, having at least a three-month stockpile of food and water, along with essentials like medicine and hygiene products, is a good starting point. But it's not enough to stop at that. Keep adding to your stockpile and learning new skills to be always prepared for any situation. Remember, having knowledge is a powerful tool - keep informed and be safe!
