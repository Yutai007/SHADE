Many preppers are familiar with lifeboats, but how much supplies should one have? It's crucial to have a preparedness plan and stockpile supplies to survive a SHTF scenario, but figuring out what to include can be daunting. In my experience, having at least a three-month stockpile of food and water, and a variety of essentials like medicine and hygiene products, is a good starting point. But it's crucial to keep adding to your stash and learning new skills to stay prepared for any scenario. Stay informed and stay prepared!
