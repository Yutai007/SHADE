Many preppers are familiar with lifeboats, but how much supplies should one have? It's crucial to have a preparedness plan and stockpile supplies to survive a SHTF scenario, but figuring out what to include can be daunting. In my experience, having at least a three-month stockpile of food and water, and essential supplies like medicine and hygiene products, can be a good starting point. However, don't stop at that! Don't be afraid to add to your stockpile and learn new skills to be always prepared for any situation. Always remember, knowledge can be a powerful tool - stay informed and stay safe!
