Azaleas unfurl in blush, beneath pecan trees,
Magnolias bloom, carried on a gentle breeze.
Rivers carry pollen, pine essence fills the air,
Confederate jasmine vines, sweet fragrance spreads.

Redbud trees in bloom, a clear Carolina blue,
Gentle Spanish moss, a gentle ethereal view.
Cardinals in a fiery blush, a passionate red,
A deer's gentle footsteps, camellia petals spread.

Wisteria a blanket, lilac a fair view,
Buttercups in the field, a golden amber light.
Mockingbirds in a southern live oak shade,
A quartet of melody in a southern spring.

The song of the cicadas echoes, a sultry day,
Fireflies in the dusk, a gentle light rush.
Humid nights, a silver moon, a sweet tea day,
A southern spring, a spell, a southern charm.

Its warmth seeps into your bones, like sweet tea,
Whispers of a bygone era, hoop skirts sway.
Beneath the lazy sun, the south unfurls her spring,
A silent invitation, a southern enchantment.
