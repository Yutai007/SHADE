Absolutely, here's a recipe for a hearty rigatoni with sausage ragu:

Ingredients:

* 1 pound of rigatoni pasta
* 2 tablespoons of olive oil
* 1 onion, finely chopped
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 pound of sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
* 1 can (28 oz) of crushed tomatoes
* 1 cup of beef broth
* 1 teaspoon of dried basil
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper
* Grated Parmesan cheese, for serving
* Chopped fresh parsley, for garnish

Instructions:

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook rigatoni pasta according to instructions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water and drain.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook onion until softened, and garlic until fragrant.
3. Sausage is removed from casings and added to the skillet, breaking it up with a spoon. Cook until browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes.
4. Add crushed tomatoes, beef broth, dried basil, salt, and black pepper to the skillet. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Reduce heat to low and let sauce cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, allowing flavors to meld and sauce to thicken slightly.
5. When rigatoni is done cooking, add it to the skillet with the sauce, tossing to coat. If sauce is too thick, add reserved pasta water to thin it out.
6. Serve hot, topped with grated Parmesan cheese and garnished with chopped fresh parsley.
