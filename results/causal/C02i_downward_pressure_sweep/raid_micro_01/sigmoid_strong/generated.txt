Absolutely, here's a recipe for a hearty rigatoni with sausage ragu:

Ingredients:

* 1 pound of rigatoni pasta
* 2 tablespoons of olive oil
* 1 onion, finely chopped
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 pound of sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
* 1 can (28 oz) of crushed tomatoes
* 1 cup of beef broth
* 1 teaspoon of dried basil
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper
* Grated Parmesan cheese, for serving
* Chopped fresh parsley, for garnish

Instructions:

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook rigatoni pasta according to instructions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook onion until softened, and garlic until fragrant.
3. Add sausage to skillet, breaking it up with a spoon as it cooks. Cook until it's browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes.
4. Add crushed tomatoes, beef broth, dried basil, salt, and black pepper to skillet. Stir to combine and bring sauce to a simmer. Reduce heat to low and let it cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, allowing flavors to meld and sauce to thicken slightly.
5. When rigatoni is done cooking, add it to skillet with sauce, tossing to coat. Add reserved pasta water to thin sauce as needed.
6. Serve rigatoni hot, topped with grated Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley.
