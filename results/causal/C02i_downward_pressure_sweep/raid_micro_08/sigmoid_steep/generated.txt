The Extended LTLvis Motion Planning Interface (Extended Technical Report) introduces an enhancement to the LTLvis motion planning tool, which is a popular software for robotic motion control. This update includes new functionalities like non-linear motion planning, collision detection, and path optimization. The paper offers a comprehensive overview of the extended interface, detailing its architecture, algorithms, and implementation. It also showcases a variety of motion planning scenarios, illustrating the interface's effectiveness. Overall, this report serves as a significant resource for both researchers and practitioners in the robotic motion planning domain.
