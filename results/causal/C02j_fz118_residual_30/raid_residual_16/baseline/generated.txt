Edwin Bryant (January 1834 – April 4, 1908) was an English-born American businessman and politician who served as the sixth mayor of Los Angeles, California, from 1884 to 1886. In 1857, Bryant moved to California and settled in Los Angeles. He founded the Los Angeles Printing Press and served as its editor and publisher. He also worked as a journalist and wrote for various newspapers and magazines.

Bryant was elected to the Los Angeles Common Council in 1872 and served as a councilman for two terms. He was elected mayor in 1884 and served for two years. During his tenure, he advocated for the development of the city's water system and the expansion of its public transportation system.
