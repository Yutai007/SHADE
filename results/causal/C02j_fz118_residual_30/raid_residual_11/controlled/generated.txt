Borussia Dortmund is facing financial difficulties, according to a report in the German newspaper Bild.

The Bundesliga club, which has won the German title twice in the past three seasons, is said to be in debt to the tune of 100 million euros (£80 million).

The club's chief executive, Hans-Joachim Watzke, denies the report, saying: "We are not in danger of going bust."

Dortmund currently ranks third in the Bundesliga, 10 points behind leaders Bayern Munich.

The club's debts have been exacerbated by a 100 million euro loan taken out in 2008 to help fund the construction of a new stadium.

The global economic crisis has hit the club's sponsors and commercial partners, further exacerbating the situation.
