The Science of Discworld blends two parallel storylines: one in the fantastical Discworld, a flat planet with four elephants and a giant turtle, and the other in the real world, focusing on a science experiment at the Unseen University in Ankh-Morpork. In Discworld, wizards accidentally create universes within bottles, leading to revelations about their own universe's principles and the power of narrative. Meanwhile, science writers Ian Stewart and Jack Cohen explore Discworld's landscapes, explaining complex science in accessible ways with real-world examples. The novel weaves science and fiction, challenging common paradigms and exploring the role of imagination, stories, and belief in the universe.
