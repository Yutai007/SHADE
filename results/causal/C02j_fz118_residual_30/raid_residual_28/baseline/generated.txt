A night in June

In June, I was with you,

Afraid of love.

This poem captures the fear of a one-nightstand; it's common, but the girl/woman is afraid of love.

The next selection, "My Only Love" from Poetry in Song, vol. 2, was inspired by the line: "You are the only love that lasted all my life." The poem was dedicated to my late father, who passed away on June 12, 2010. Born in Jamaica and living in East Elmhurst, Queens, and Brooklyn, NY, my father was the most amazing teacher and mentor. He taught me about life and encouraged me to be part of my community. I often thought about what it would be like to be an old man in a nursing home. It was God's will.

My Only Love

Do not grieve for me on the day that I am laid to rest,

In my old age, my soul had finally come to rest.

Now that the end had come, I'm now satisfied,

I lived a full life,

And now I have found peace.

In my past, there are many things I regret,

It is now the time to forgive and forget.

While they are mourning at my funeral,

Tell them I'll always be their true friend,

My only love that would always make me happy,

Was when you'd come to see me so we could take a nap.

When I am laid to rest, please do me the favor,

Take one more look upon my face,

The very same face,

That made me the man I became!

I always knew that I would travel on the right road,

Because you always made sure that everyone was alright.

Your children knew that you were always there with us,

From the bottom of my heart, there you were,

But your heart was so big, that it kept going,

It never stopped.

I love you with all my heart...
