Rogier Koordes, born on August 4, 1964, in Amsterdam, is a Dutch composer and musician. At the age of five, the piano played a significant role in his life. After playing classical music for twelve years, he gradually switched to jazz in his late teens. In 1986, he was awarded a jazz piano scholarship by the Royal Dutch Conservatory in Amsterdam. Here, he started composing music and formed the Rogier Koordes Trio, with bassist Joris Roelofs and drummer Onno Muijs.

In 1990, he started collaborating and recording with several other musicians, including Dutch jazz musicians Benjamin Herman, Jesse van Ruller, and Cees Slinger, and American jazz musicians David Liebman, Joe Lovano, and Michael Brecker. He has performed with his own trio all over Europe and once in New York. Since 1997, he has been working as a composer for Dutch National Television, creating music for numerous television programs. His musical Yellow Cold is commissioned by the Dutch Ballet and has been performed numerous times in The Netherlands since 2004.

In 2011, Koordes released the album "Songs for Fathers," for which he wrote lyrics for the first time in Dutch.
