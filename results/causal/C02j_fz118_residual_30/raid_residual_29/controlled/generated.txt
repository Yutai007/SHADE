Herbed Egg 'Pasta' with Blood Orange Salsa and Lettuce Slaw Recipe

Ingredients:

- 4 large eggs
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 blood oranges, peeled and segmented
- 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tablespoon fresh mint, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 8 large leaves of butter lettuce, torn into bite-sized pieces
- 1 avocado, diced

Instructions:

1. In a mixing bowl, beat the eggs, salt, pepper, basil, and oregano until well combined.

2. Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet, swirling to coat the bottom of the pan and cook for 2-3 minutes until the eggs start to set.

3. Use a spatula to gently lift and fold the eggs, continue to cook for another 2-3 minutes until the eggs are cooked through but still slightly runny.

4. In a separate bowl, combine the red onion, blood orange segments, red bell pepper, cherry tomatoes, parsley, mint, and lime juice to make the blood orange salsa.

5. In another bowl, combine the butter lettuce and diced avocado to make the lettuce slaw.

6. Divide the egg 'pasta' onto plates, spoon the blood orange salsa over the top, and serve with the lettuce slaw on the side.

7. Garnish with extra parsley and mint if desired.

Enjoy your delicious and healthy Herbed Egg 'Pasta' with Blood Orange Salsa and Lettuce Slaw!
