In the mirror, I see her face,
A reflection of my own, a familiar grace.
The same eyes, the same nose, the same mouth,
A family resemblance, without a doubt.

Her hands, they hold mine, so tight,
A gentle touch, a loving sight.
She whispers words of love and care,
A symphony of comfort, beyond compare.

Her smile, it lights up the room,
A ray of sunshine, a beacon of gloom.
Her laughter, it rings out loud,
A joyful sound, a happy crowd.

In her arms, I find my home,
A place of safety, a place to roam.
She guides me through life's ups and downs,
A constant presence, a steady crown.

With every step, I grow and learn,
A journey she's been on, she'll always yearn.
She teaches me to be strong and brave,
To stand up tall, to never be a slave.

Through good times and bad, she's always there,
A rock, a shelter, a love so rare.
She's more than just my mother, she's my friend,
A bond that will never end.

So here's to you, dear mother of mine,
A poem to express my love and find.
In you, I see a woman of grace,
A mother and daughter, a perfect embrace.
