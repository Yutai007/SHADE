"American Hustle" is a film about con artists and the people they deceive. Set in the late 1970s and early 1980s, it is based on a true story. The film stars Christian Bale, Amy Adams, Bradley Cooper, Jennifer Lawrence, and Jeremy Renner.

Directed by David O. Russell and written by Eric Warren Singer and David O. Russell, the film was released on December 20, 2013, and received positive reviews from critics. It was nominated for ten Academy Awards and won three Oscars, while also being nominated for five Golden Globe Awards and winning two Golden Globes.

"American Hustle" is a great film. The acting is superb, the story is intriguing, and the film is well-made. I highly recommend this film.
