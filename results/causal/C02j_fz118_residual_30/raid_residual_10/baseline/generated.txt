In "Cold is the Grave," the seventh book in Peter Robinson's Inspector Banks series, Detective Chief Inspector Alan Banks faces his most challenging case yet. The novel begins with a disturbing discovery: Kyle Heath, a troubled teenager, is found dead in a homemade grave on the moors. His cryptic clues hint at a sinister motive, leading Banks to investigate a sinister web of secrets and lies within the tight-knit community of Eastvale.

Kyle's troubled past and his association with the Submitting Church, a local religious sect, raise suspicions. Banks suspects that his death was not just random but part of a larger conspiracy. Detective Inspector Annie Cabbot, Banks's loyal partner, feels a personal connection to the case due to her own turbulent past. Her determination to find justice for Kyle leads her down a dangerous path, and she confronts her own demons.

As the investigation progresses, Banks and Cabbot realize that there are people who will go to great lengths to protect the secrets buried in Eastvale's darkest corners. The tension escalates, and the detectives race against time to catch the killer, who seems to always be one step ahead. "Cold is the Grave" is a chilling and suspenseful novel that explores the darker side of human nature. Robinson's intricate plotting and sharp characterization bring the small town of Eastvale to life, making it a haunting backdrop for the complex and unsettling storyline. As Banks and Cabbot navigate a labyrinth of deception, the true nature of evil is revealed, leaving readers on the edge of their seats until the shocking conclusion.
