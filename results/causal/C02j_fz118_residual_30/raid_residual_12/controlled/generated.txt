A Dream of Foxes  
by Niki de Saint Phalle
I was afraid of being alone
and I called the foxes to watch me.
They came and licked me and said:
"It is true you are just a body
no more a goddess
but you will not be lonely."
And I said,
"No, I will not be lonely."
And a fox bit my finger
and showed me his fangs and said:
"It is true your body is not made of gold
but it is still shining."
And I said,
"No, my body is not shining."
And a fox bit my arm
and showed me blood
and said:
"The moon is full and shining like your body
as the stars are bright like your eyes."
And I said,
"No, my eyes are not bright like the stars."
And I cried.
And the foxes were happy,
because they liked watching
your tears while crying.
They resemble stars, eyes, or bodies.
Their shadows shine with scars.
