"Ingredients:

1/2 cup butter

1/2 cup white sugar

1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder

1/2 cup all-purpose flour

1/2 cup chopped walnuts

1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips

1/2 cup white chocolate chips

1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk

1/2 cup instant coffee granules

1/2 cup confectioners' sugar

Directions:

Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x9 inch pan.

In a medium bowl, cream together the butter, sugar and cocoa powder. Mix in the flour and walnuts. Press into the bottom of the prepared pan.

Bake for 15 minutes in the preheated oven. Remove from oven and sprinkle with chocolate chips. Return to oven for 1 minute, or until chocolate chips are soft. Spread chocolate chips evenly over the crust.

In a small saucepan, combine the white chocolate chips, condensed milk and instant coffee. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until smooth. Pour over the chocolate chips. Refrigerate until firm.

Remove from refrigerator and sprinkle with confectioners' sugar. Cut into bars."
