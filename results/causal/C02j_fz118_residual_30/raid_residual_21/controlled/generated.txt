Ingredients:
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 3 large eggs
- Oil for frying (vegetable or peanut)

Instructions:

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and pat them dry with paper towels to remove any moisture. This ensures the chicken pieces are dry and crispy.

2. In one bowl, mix the panko breadcrumbs and garlic powder. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.

3. In another bowl, beat three eggs until they are well combined. Make sure there are no lumps in the eggs.

4. Take each piece of chicken and dip it into the beaten eggs, coating it evenly. Shake off any excess eggs and then dip it into the panko mixture. Shake off any excess breadcrumbs and place the coated chicken on a plate or dish.

5. Heat the oil in a medium-high heat pan until it reaches 325°F (using a thermometer, check by dropping a small bit of breadcrumb into the oil; if it sizzles, the oil is ready). Reduce the heat to low-medium and fry the chicken pieces until they are golden brown. Remove the fried chicken from the pan onto a kitchen towel-lined plate to drain any excess oil. Serve hot and enjoy!
