Ingredients:
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 3 large eggs
- Oil for frying (vegetable or peanut)

Instructions:

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and pat them dry with paper towels to remove any moisture. This ensures the karaage stays crispy!

2. In one bowl, mix together the panko breadcrumbs and garlic powder. Season with salt and black pepper to your preference.

3. In another separate bowl, beat three eggs until well combined.

4. Dip each piece of chicken into the beaten eggs, ensuring they are evenly coated. Shake off any excess egg from all sides.

5. Coat each piece of chicken in the panko mixture, shaking off any excess. Place the coated chicken on a meal prep container or any other dish as you do with the rest of the chicken pieces. Make sure every part is covered properly with breadcrumbs.

6. Heat oil in a medium-high heat pan until it reaches 325°F (using a thermometer, check by dropping a small bit of breadcrumb; if it sizzles, the oil is ready). Reduce the flame to low-medium slow cook the chicken until it turns golden brown. Remove the fried karaages onto a kitchen towel-lined platter to drain any excess oil. Serve hot and enjoy!
