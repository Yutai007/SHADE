The novel chronicles the story of two women, Isabel and Julia, who reside in Boston and share a passionate, intimate relationship. Despite the societal norms of the time, which discourage the idea of women being together, Isabel and Julia are devoted to each other and have a deep love for one another.

However, their relationship is put to the test when Isabel becomes engaged to a man named Arthur. Although she doesn't love him, Isabel feels pressured to accept his proposal because of her age and her desire to have a secure future. Julia, on the other hand, is determined to win Isabel back and fights for their relationship.

As the novel progresses, Isabel begins to realize that her relationship with Julia is the real source of her happiness. She breaks off her engagement with Arthur and returns to Julia, embracing a future that society has not yet accepted. Despite the challenges they face, Isabel and Julia's love proves to be stronger than the expectations of others, and their relationship becomes a testament to the power of love and loyalty.
