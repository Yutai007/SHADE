The novel chronicles the story of two women, Isabel and Julia, who reside in Boston and share a passionate, intimate relationship. Despite the societal norms of the time, which discourage the idea of women being together, Isabel and Julia are devoted to each other with deep love.

However, their relationship is tested when Isabel becomes engaged to a man named Arthur. Although she doesn't love him, Isabel feels pressured to accept his proposal due to her age and her desire to have a secure future. Julia, determined to win Isabel back, fights for their relationship.

As the novel progresses, Isabel begins to realize that her relationship with Julia is the real source of her happiness. She breaks off her engagement with Arthur and returns to Julia, embracing a future society has not yet accepted. Despite facing challenges, Isabel and Julia's love proves stronger than others' expectations, making their relationship a testament to the power of love and loyalty.
