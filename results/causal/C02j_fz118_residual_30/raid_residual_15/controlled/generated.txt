This movie was fantastic. It's been a long time since I went to the movies and left feeling like I got my money's worth. I'm a fan of all the Superman movies, TV shows, and comic books. "Man of Steel" brought back a sense of youthful glee; it awakened the inner 5-year-old in all of us. I'm not sure if it was the acting, the special effects, or the fact that as a fan, I cheered on Superman/Clark Kent (Henry Cavill) as he made his way through the movie, but this movie delivered. There were action scenes throughout the movie that were nothing short of spectacular. Kostroma, Russia, and the Philippines disaster scenes were bone-chilling and hair-raising. As Superman rescued the workers from the collapsing building, it was like watching your favorite superhero come to life. This movie also posed questions of moral responsibility and the accountability that comes with possessing extraordinary powers. "Man of Steel" is a must-see for all fans, and a great introduction for those who are not. I plan on seeing this one again while it's still in the theater. Go see this movie, you won't be disappointed.
