This paper examines the downlink transmission of multiple cells employing coordinated beamforming with rate outage constraints to maximize the minimum throughput among all users. We utilize block diagonalization to ensure multicell throughput fairness while minimizing the computational complexity of determining the optimal beamforming vectors. We derive new analytical expressions for downlink Signal-to-Interference-plus-Noise Ratio (SINR) and rate for each user in the cell-range-expONENT domain. We then offer novel semi-closed-form expressions to optimally solve the beamforming problem with computational complexity of O(MG), where G is the number of cells and M is the number of users per cell. Our extensive simulations confirm the accuracy of the proposed expressions and highlight the algorithm's advantages over existing methods, especially in scenarios with a large number of users. The complexity analysis of the proposed algorithm is also provided.
