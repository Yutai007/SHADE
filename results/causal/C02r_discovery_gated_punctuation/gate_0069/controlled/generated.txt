This paper explores resource allocation in MIMO radar systems with multiple targets for non-coherent localization. The problem is addressed as an optimization problem to minimize the mean squared error in localization. The study examines the effects of key system parameters, including the number of transmit and receive antennas, the number of transmitted signals, and the sampling rate, on radar system performance. An efficient algorithm based on the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) is proposed to solve the optimization problem. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed resource allocation scheme significantly enhances the radar system's localization accuracy compared to conventional methods.
