In the futuristic world of En Iniya Iyanthira, Sujatha Rangarajan's science fiction novel follows the journey of Raghunath, a brilliant scientist who invents a revolutionary device called Iyanthira. This device has the power to control and manipulate the human mind. However, Raghunath's invention falls into the wrong hands when a powerful corporation, led by the ruthless businessman, Varadarajan, steals the technology. Raghunath becomes a fugitive, hunted by Varadarajan's henchmen. The novel follows Raghunath as he embarks on a dangerous journey to reclaim his invention and expose the corporation's sinister plans. He forms an alliance with a group of rebels who are fighting against the oppressive regime. As the story unfolds, Raghunath discovers the true power of Iyanthira and its ability to awaken the dormant powers within individuals. The novel explores the themes of power, corruption, and the ethical implications of advanced technology. It delves into the moral dilemmas faced by the characters as they grapple with the consequences of their actions. En Iniya Iyanthira is a gripping and thought-provoking tale that keeps readers on the edge of their seats, questioning the boundaries of science and the human mind.
