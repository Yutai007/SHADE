On October 2, 1834, Julien Sorel, a poor watchmaker's apprentice from Verrières, Franche-Comté, recounts his tale to the local curé, Abbé Chélan.

Sorel, in love with Mélanie, the beautiful daughter of a wealthy Verrières family, has a secret love with Mme de Rênal, the married school mistress he works for. Mme de Rênal discovers the affair and Sorel is forced to leave Verrières. He works for a Besançon watchmaker, where he meets and falls deeply in love with Mme de Wolmar, a rich widow.

Returning home, they reconcile, but the 1848 Revolution forces him to flee. Eventually, he returns to France, settling in Paris, where he dies of gunshot wounds.
