This paper introduces the concept of witness structures and immediate snapshot complexes as a method to analyze the combinatorial structure of simplicial complexes. Witness structures are an extension of simplicial complexes that allow for representation of higher-dimensional combinatorial entities. Immediate snapshot complexes specifically capture the structural characteristics at a particular moment in time. The document outlines algorithms for constructing these structures along with techniques for examining their properties. The authors illustrate the practical applications of these concepts through examples from social networks and biological systems. In summary, this paper presents a novel approach to studying the combinatorial structure of complex systems, offering new perspectives on their behavior.
