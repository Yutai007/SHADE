1 cup softened butter

2 and 1/4 cups granulated sugar

3 tablespoons cornstarch (or arrowroot powder)

1 teaspoon baking soda (optional but recommended)

1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional, does not taste like cookies!)

3 sticks softened unsalted butter

1 large brownie

1 cup softened cream cheese

3/4 cup white flour

1 tablespoon orange juice (optional, replace with 2 cups water or orange zest)

6 ounces softened shortening

In a large mixing bowl, combine butter, sugar, cornstarch, baking soda, and vanilla extract. Mix well until mixture resembles coarse chocolate chunks. Roll mixture out on a parchment paper-covered baking sheet, leaving a 1-inch border on each sheet. Bake in a preheated and increased oven until done (about 12 minutes). Allow cookies to cool on a baking sheet until completely cool and then remove and cool on a baking sheet. Makes 12 small (about 11x 5") cookies.
