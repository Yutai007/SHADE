This paper examines the issue of power flow in electrical circuits, specifically focusing on situations where power surges occur in the opposite direction to traditional flow – a phenomenon known as inverse power flow. This unconventional problem has gained significance due to the increasing integration of distributed generation units, such as solar panels, into power systems. The study introduces a comprehensive mathematical model, including constraints on voltage and power limits, and proposes an efficient solution method based on optimization algorithms to determine the pattern of inverse power flow. Case studies in this research illustrate the practical implications and potential for the management of power systems, and pave the way for more precise and efficient manipulation of power flow in complex electrical grids. Future implications of managing inverse power flow and its importance in the development of smart grid technologies are also explored.
