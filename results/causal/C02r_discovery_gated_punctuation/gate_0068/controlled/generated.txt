Montana Technological University, commonly known as Montana Tech, Tech, or MTU, is a public university situated in Butte, Montana, United States. Founded in 1900 as the Montana State School of Mines, it became affiliated with the University of Montana in 1994. Montana Tech offers bachelor's and master's degrees in engineering and science, specializing notably in mining engineering and petroleum engineering.

Originally established to train miners and metallurgical engineers after the closure of the University of Montana's School of Mines, Montana Tech was renamed the Montana College of Mineral Science and Technology in 1965 and later changed its name again to Montana Technological University in 1986.

Accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities, Montana Tech provides undergraduate and graduate programs in engineering and sciences, particularly focusing on mining engineering and petroleum engineering.

Located within the University of Montana System, Montana Tech has an enrollment size of approximately 2,500 students.
