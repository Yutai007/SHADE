Paulinho, who has been on a year-long deal, was ruled out of the game against his former club on Sunday and could be out against West Brom on Sunday as well. However, his position could change if he is forced off in the last couple of minutes of the game.

Paulinho sustained an injury while playing for the reserves against Liverpool on Sunday night. According to a statement from his Instagram account, "I have a problem with my knee. I won't be able to play anymore. Thanks for all love by the world. Go Brazil!!!"

Paulinho is expected to be a crucial player for Spurs in England this season, and he should have no trouble replacing Levy's recent replacement Mousa Dembele.
