I recently installed a motion sensing switch in my home and wanted to share a solution I found to set it up for only certain hours. I wanted the switch to be active during evening and night, but not during the day. After some research, I discovered that many motion sensing switches have a feature called "time delay adjustment" or "timeout setting" that allows you to control how long the switch stays on after detecting motion. By adjusting this setting, you can effectively limit the switch's operation to specific hours. I found the instructions for adjusting the time delay in the user manual of my switch, but it may vary depending on the brand and model. Overall, it's a simple and convenient solution to customize the operation of a motion sensing switch according to your needs.
