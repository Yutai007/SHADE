> 8-1/2 cups water, divided
> 4 small carrots, chopped
> 3 stalks celery, diced
> 4 cloves garlic, minced
> 2 leeks (white part only), cut into pieces
> 6 (6 oz.) packages fresh baby spinach leaves
> One 4 lb turkey, cooked and diced or shredded
> 8 tomatos (or equivalent amount frozen tomatoes)
> 2 tsp dried rosemary
> 2 bay leaves
> Salt & Pepper to taste
> Corn meal noodles: combine in food processor mix 1 cup cornmeal, 1 teaspoon salt, and 4 cups hot tap water; pulse mixture until dough comes together then set aside for 5 minutes. Dust countertop with flour dusted with potato starch or powder sugar. Roll out cornmeal dough as if rolling out pie crust. Cut in strips either by hand or scissors about an inch thick. Bring each strip up at one end like you were making lasagna. Cover pan lightly. Set apart while working on vegetable soup and cooking your broth/stock. Cooking broth: In a large stockpot melt coconut oil over medium heat. Saute garlic til light golden being careful not to burn it. Add remaining vegetables including herbs bring to boil reduce heat cover and simmer till tender about 20-30 minutes. Strain through colander discard solids - use juice for base for soup if desired add more filtered water, organic chicken base and season lightly add meat. Put noodle into bowl arrange vegetables place meat into center put broth around the edge. Garnish with additional salt pepper and parsley serve immediately.
