> 8-1/2 cups water, divided
> 4 small carrots, chopped
> 3 stalks celery, diced
> 4 cloves garlic, minced
> 2 leeks (white part only), cut into pieces
> 6 (6 oz.) packages fresh baby spinach leaves
> One 4 lb turkey, cooked and diced or shredded
> 8 tomatos (or equivalent amount frozen tomatoes)
> 2 tsp dried rosemary
> 2 bay leaves
> Salt & Pepper to taste
> Corn meal noodles: combine in food processor mix 1 cup cornmeal, 1 teaspoon salt, and 4 cups hot tap water; pulse mixture until dough comes together then set aside for 5 minutes. Dust countertop with flour and corn starch. Roll out dough as if rolling out pie crust. Cut in strips either by hand or scissors about an inch thick. Bring each strip up at one end like you were making lasagna. Cover pan lightly. Set apart while working on vegetable soup and cooking your broth/stock. Cooking broth: In a large stock pot melt coconut oil over medium heat. Saute garlic until light golden, being careful not to burn it. Add remaining vegetables including herbs, bring to boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer until tender, about 20-30 minutes. Strain through a colander, discard solids, and use juice for soup if desired. Add more filtered water, organic chicken base, and season lightly. Add meat. Put noodles into a bowl, arrange vegetables, and place meat in the center. Put broth around the edge. Garnish with additional salt, pepper, and parsley. Serve immediately.
