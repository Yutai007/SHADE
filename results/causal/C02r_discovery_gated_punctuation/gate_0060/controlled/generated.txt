A quick update: My wife and I both bought our homes in 2005. Our homes are our most valuable assets and we own them outright. We've been through some painful times over the past two years but have tried our best to not lose our assets. We will always have our home, so we made some tough decisions for ourselves, and it cost us to keep our house. We have also been very fortunate to find good jobs during this entire crisis that have allowed us to save and keep our jobs. Our jobs have also allowed us a certain degree of flexibility to make some tough choices; not something everyone has had the opportunity to do. All in all we are fortunate we are in no jeopardy of losing our homes or losing our savings. Still have some tough road to travel and could be years before things fully recover but we have a plan and are optimistic.
