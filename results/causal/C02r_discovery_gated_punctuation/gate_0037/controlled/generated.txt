Flemish Beef Roast (Or Stew) with Vegetables Recipe

Ingredients:
- 1 2-3 pound beef roast, trimmed
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 cup red wine
- 2 cups chopped mushrooms
- Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a large Dutch oven or pot over medium-high heat. Add beef roast and brown on all sides, about 8 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
3. Add chopped onion, carrots, celery, garlic, thyme, and rosemary to pot. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are softened.
4. Stir in tomato paste, beef broth, red wine, and mushrooms. Bring to a simmer.
5. Season beef roast with salt and pepper. Place roast back in pot, spooning vegetable mixture on top.
6. Cover pot with a lid and transfer to preheated oven. Roast for about 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until beef is tender and vegetables are cooked through.
7. Remove roast from pot and let rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving. Serve with vegetable mixture on the side. Enjoy!
