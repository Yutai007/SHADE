Abstract: The atomic layer deposition (ALD) process has been employed to create a series of layers on a substrate, which can serve as a substrate for photonics circuits. However, this method necessitates the substrate to be prepared beforehand and coated with a thin layer of a metal oxide or other material. A new method has been introduced to fabricate such a substrate by depositing a single layer of atomic layer deposited (ALD) second-order nonlinear optical metamaterials (NOLMs). This ALD NOLM can be integrated into a CMOS-compatible chip using a conventional silicon wafer. This approach enables the creation of a high-performance, low-power, and compact device.
