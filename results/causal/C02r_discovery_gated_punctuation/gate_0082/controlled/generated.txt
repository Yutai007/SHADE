Tibor Grasser, born in 1969, is an Austrian composer and pianist. He studied piano at the Vienna Conservatory with Karl Leister and composition with Friedrich Cerha.

Grasser's music is characterized by its use of dissonance, rhythmic complexity, and a wide range of dynamics. He has written works for solo piano, chamber ensembles, orchestras, and opera. His music has been performed by ensembles such as the Vienna Philharmonic Orchestra, the Kölner Ensemble, and the Ensemble Modern.

In addition to his work as a composer, Grasser is an accomplished pianist and has performed with artists such as Gidon Kremer and Yehudi Menuhin. He has recorded several albums of his own music, including "Piano Concerto No. 1" and "Symphony No. 1."

Grasser's music has been praised for its emotional intensity and its ability to evoke a wide range of emotions. He has won numerous awards for his compositions, including the Ernst von Siemens Music Prize and the Joseph Haydn Prize.
