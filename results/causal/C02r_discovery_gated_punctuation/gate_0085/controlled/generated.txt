Ingredients:
- 1 head of broccoli, cut into small florets
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup of ricotta cheese
- 1/4 cup of grated parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup of hot honey
- 8 slices of bread
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil

Instructions:

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F.

2. In a large bowl, mix together the broccoli florets, minced garlic, olive oil, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

3. Spread the broccoli mixture onto a baking sheet and roast in the oven for 20-25 minutes, until the broccoli is tender and slightly browned.

4. While the broccoli is roasting, prepare the ricotta mixture. In a small bowl, mix together the ricotta cheese, parmesan cheese, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

5. Once the broccoli is done, remove it from the oven and reduce the temperature to 375°F.

6. Toast the bread slices in the oven for 5-7 minutes, until lightly golden.

7. Remove the bread from the oven and spread a thin layer of ricotta mixture onto each slice.

8. Top each slice of bread with a generous spoonful of roasted broccoli and a drizzle of hot honey.

9. Serve immediately and enjoy delicious Broccoli and Garlic-Ricotta Toasts with Hot Honey!
