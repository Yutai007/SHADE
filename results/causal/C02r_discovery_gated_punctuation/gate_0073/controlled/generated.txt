Leonti Mroveli, born on January 29, 1978, in Tbilisi, Georgia, is a poet and musician of Georgian-Armenian descent. He grew up in Batumi, Georgia, on the Black Sea coast. In an interview with Bidzina Kheiladze, Mroveli revealed that his maternal grandfather, Shalva Mroveli, had migrated from Armenia in the Soviet era. His paternal grandparents had been forced from Nakhchivan due to Soviet atrocities against the Mroveli family. Mroveli began composing his own songs from a young age, often incorporating his own experiences of being a refugee in his music. He drew inspiration from a variety of musical styles, including pop rock, jazz, and blues. Mroveli is a musician with a strong sense of his own identity, and his songs often reflect on societal issues in Georgia, including poverty, inequality, and political unrest. He has performed in Georgia and has also made appearances in other countries.
