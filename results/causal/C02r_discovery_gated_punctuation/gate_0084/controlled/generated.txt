Protests have erupted in Algiers over the government's decision to halt gas and oil production at the Ain Salah field. Thousands of people gathered in the capital to voice their opposition, with demonstrations also taking place in other cities. The government suspended production at the site last week, citing discord among different interests. Saturday's protests were largely peaceful, but clashes erupted in Boumerdes, east of Algiers. The oil and gas sector is crucial to Algeria's economy, alongside agriculture. However, the country has faced challenges in maintaining prices for its exports due to falling energy prices and increased competition from Russia and the US. The decision to halt production at Ain Salah was part of a strategy to save money by reducing output at less profitable sites.
