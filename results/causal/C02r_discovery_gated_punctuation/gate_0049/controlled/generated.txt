Asparagus with Prosciutto di Parma, Pecorino Pepato, and Poached Eggs

Ingredients:

1 bunch asparagus (about 1 pound)
6 large eggs
4 tablespoons unsalted butter
4 thin slices prosciutto di Parma, torn into pieces
1/2 cup grated Pecorino Pepato cheese
1/2 cup heavy cream
1/2 teaspoon white pepper
1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions:

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil over high heat. Meanwhile, fill a large bowl with ice water and set aside. Cut the tips of the asparagus and set aside. Cut the stalks into 1-inch pieces and set aside. Blanch the tips in boiling water for 1 minute. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the tips to the ice water and chill for 1 minute. Drain, pat dry, and set aside. Blanch the stalks for 4 to 6 minutes or until tender. Drain, pat dry, and set aside.
2. Crack the eggs into individual ramekins or cups. Place a small amount of cold tap water at each end of an egg yolk and gently slide it into a pan filled with simmering water. Cook for 3 to 5 minutes or until the whites are set and the yolks are cooked as desired. Use a slotted spoon to remove the eggs from the water and let them cool on paper towels.
3. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the prosciutto and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, or until crisp. Add the asparagus and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, or until tender-crisp. Stir in 1 tablespoon of melted butter, then sprinkle 2 teaspoons of Pecorino Pepato cheese over the vegetables. Continue cooking for 1 minute, or until all the cheese is melted. Stir in 4 ounces (2 sticks) of heavy cream, season with 1/2 teaspoon black pepper and 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and simmer for 1 minute, or until heated through.
4. To serve, divide the asparagus and prosciutto among individual plates. Top each with a poached egg and some of the cheese sauce.
