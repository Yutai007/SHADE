Richard Queen, born in 1958 in Arlington, Virginia, is an American politician and businessman who has served as a member of the Virginia House of Delegates since 2004. He represents the 67th district, which includes parts of Fairfax County in Northern Virginia.

Queen's early life and education

Queen was born on July 3, 1958, in Arlington, Virginia. He attended George Mason University, where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration in 1980.

Career

After graduating from college, Queen worked in the private sector before entering politics. In 1995, he was elected to the Fairfax County Board of Supervisors, representing the Braddock District. While serving on the board, he focused on transportation, public safety, and economic development.

In 2003, Queen ran for the Virginia House of Delegates, representing the newly created 67th district, which included parts of Fairfax County. He won the election with 57% of the vote, defeating Democrat John H. Riggs.

Since being elected to the House, Queen has been a strong advocate for transportation funding and infrastructure improvements. He has also been active in promoting economic growth and job creation in his district. Additionally, he has been a vocal supporter of public safety measures, including increased funding for law enforcement agencies.

Personal life
