Pineapple Cross Buns Recipe

Ingredients:
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup warm pineapple juice
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup raisins
- 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
- Zest of 1 orange
- Cross Bun Toppings
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, softened
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:

1. In a stand mixer, combine the flour, sugar, yeast, and salt. Add the warm pineapple juice, melted butter, and vanilla extract. Mix on medium speed until smooth, about 5-8 minutes.

2. Stir in the raisins, chopped walnuts, and orange zest. Continue mixing to ensure everything is evenly distributed.

3. Cover the bowl with a kitchen towel and let rise in a warm, draft-free place for 1 hour or until doubled in size.

4. Punch down the dough and shape into 12 equal portions. Roll each into a ball and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet, about 2 inches apart.

5. Let the dough rise for 30 minutes or until nearly doubled in size.

6. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

7. For the cross buns, use a sharp knife to make a cross on the top of each bun.

8. In a small bowl, mix powdered sugar, milk, and softened butter. Brush this mixture over the top of each bun.

9. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown.
