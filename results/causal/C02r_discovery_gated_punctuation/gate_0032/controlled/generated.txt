I've worked in AAA retail and online distribution companies, including during peak years for releases like GTA 4 and Black Ops 2. I know it's past that now. I've worked on BF1-4, AC Syndicates, and Unity. So many games this year were horrible—Battlefront 2017 wasn't much better than it's launch issues. It was like it was getting worse with every update, including not even acknowledging that some things were broken. This was a problem for players who had to figure out workarounds or even worse, had to let things go. This was even worse because it was a live service title, and servers never closed. It was 24/7 and was running while providing content drops. Because it was a patch, it was supposed to be a 1 patch, but it was taking much longer. It was like adding 50GB onto an install depending on how feature-complete builds were before sending out a pre-alpha playable demo. And it was even worse because there were release date delays, and I believed that anything that was taking longer to be done was because it was just polishing up already finished levels.
