Blue Merle was an American rock band from Los Angeles, California, United States. The band consisted of brothers Liam Finn (vocals, guitar) and Caleb Johnson (bass, keyboards), and sister Morgan Finn (drums, percussion). The band released two albums, Burning in the Sun (2003) and Keeps Me Wondering (2006), before breaking up in 2007.

Liam and Caleb Finn formed Blue Merle in 2002 after the dissolution of their previous band, the Johnson Brothers. The pair were soon joined by their sister Morgan and the three began writing and performing together.

The band released their debut album, Burning in the Sun, in 2003. The album was produced by T-Bone Burnett and featured the single "Halo".

The band's second album, Keeps Me Wondering, was released in 2006. The album was produced by Brendan O'Brien and featured the singles "Out of My Hands" and "Boxcar Racer".

The band toured extensively in support of both albums, performing with artists such as Bob Dylan, Sheryl Crow, and John Mayer.

In 2007, the band announced that they were breaking up. Liam and Caleb Finn went on to form the band N. In a 2014 interview with The Huffington Post, Liam Finn stated that Blue Merle had "run its course" and that the band was "never really meant to be a long-term thing".
