This paper introduces a novel uncertainty-aware self-ensembling (UA-SE) model for 3D left atrium segmentation in semi-supervised learning. The UA-SE model leverages correlations between neighboring voxels and employs a self-ensemble strategy to boost the representation power of semi-supervised learning. Utilizing a 3D residual UNet with an uncertainty estimation module, the model models spatial correlations and outputs an uncertainty map. Additionally, a self-ensembling module is proposed that considers both labels and uncertainties by taking into account local graph structure. The model is trained semi-supervisedly, using only a small portion of labeled data for training and leveraging the remaining unlabeled data to enhance its representation power. The proposed method is evaluated on the MICCAI 3D Slicer Tracking Challenge 2017 dataset. Experimental results show the UA-SE model's effectiveness compared to state-of-the-art semi-supervised learning methods. The code and dataset are available at https://github.com/VITA-Group/UA-SE.
