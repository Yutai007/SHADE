Pork Loin Tortilla Wraps with Roasted Vegetables and Citrus Mint Dressing Recipe:

Ingredients:

* 1 lb boneless pork loin, trimmed
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp onion powder
* 1 tsp paprika
* salt
* pepper

* 4 large flour tortillas

* 1 cup mixed roasted vegetables (bell peppers, zucchini, onions, mushrooms, eggplant)

* 1/4 cup chopped fresh mint leaves

* 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro leaves

* Juice of 1 lime

* 2 tbsp honey

* 1 tbsp olive oil

* Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C).
2. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Rub the mixture over the pork loin and season both sides with salt and pepper.
3. Place the pork loin in a baking dish and roast for 20-25 minutes or until the internal temperature reaches 145°F (63°C). Allow the pork loin to rest for 5-10 minutes before slicing.
4. In a large bowl, combine the mixed roasted vegetables, mint leaves, cilantro leaves, lime juice, honey, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Toss to combine.
5. Heat the tortillas in the oven at 150°F (65°C) for 5-10 minutes or until warm.
6. Assemble by placing one half of each warmed tortilla on a plate and spreading a generous amount of the roasted vegetable and citrus mint dressing mixture onto it, leaving 1-inch borders around edges. Slice the pork loin into thin strips and arrange them atop the vegetables.
