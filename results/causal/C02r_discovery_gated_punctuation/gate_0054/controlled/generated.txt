Tom Hanks has been announced as the lead actor in a biopic about Leonardo da Vinci, a celebrated artist and inventor from history. The movie will explore his life and work, from his early years in Italy to his later achievements in France.

Hanks is no stranger to playing real-life figures on screen, having won critical acclaim for his portrayals in "Captain Phillips" (2013) and "A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood" (2019). He also received Oscar nominations for both roles.

The yet untitled project will be directed by David Koepp, who has directed "Jurassic Park" (1998) and "Lincoln" (2012), for which he received another Oscar nomination. Filming is expected to begin next year, with a release date set for 2023.

This isn't the first time that Hollywood has attempted to bring da Vinci's story to the big screen. In 2003, Mel Gibson starred in "The Agony and the Ecstasy," and in 1972, Charlton Heston played the artist in "Leonard: A Life of Leonard Bernstein (I)." However, it remains to be seen how this latest adaptation will differentiate itself from its predecessors.
