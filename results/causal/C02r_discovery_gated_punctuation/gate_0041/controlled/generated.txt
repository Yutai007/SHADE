In the near future, technology has advanced to levels previously only imagined in science fiction. Neural implants are commonplace, allowing people to connect their brains directly to computers and access information instantly. However, this newfound ability comes with its own dangers as hackers learn to exploit these connections, causing chaos and even death.

The story follows Dr. Maya Patel, a brilliant neuroscientist recruited to help the government combat the hackers. As Maya delves deeper, she discovers the hackers are a global threat, with a specific target in their sights. With the help of a former hacker and former government informant, Maya and the hackers race to uncover the truth and stop the hackers in their tracks.

"Brain Wave" is a thrilling adventure that delves into the dangers of advanced technology and the importance of privacy and security.
