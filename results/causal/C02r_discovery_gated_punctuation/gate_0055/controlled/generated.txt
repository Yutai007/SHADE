Ingredients (Serves 1)

15 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter

4 tablespoons finely chopped onion

2 tablespoons salt

2 teaspoons granulated sugar

¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon

½ teaspoon ground nutmeg

¼ teaspoon ground clove

¼ cup all-purpose flour

1 8-ounce package yeast (3 cups total)

1 ½ cups warm water

½ cup ice

Method:

1. Mix the unsalted butter, onion, salt, sugar, and spices in a large measuring cup.

2. Pour in the yeast, then stir with a wooden spoon until combined. Let it sit for 1 to 2 hours until the dough becomes soft and rolls together.

3. Gradually add the warm water and mix until the dough is soft, about 10 to 15 minutes.

4. Remove the dough and place it in a container for dough, cover with plastic wrap, and let it sit at room temperature for 8 to 12 hours, or overnight (I left mine in my draft-free kitchen for about 4 hours). The dough will come back up to room temperature and the moisture will become liquid again. Do not refrigerate the dough.

5. Remove the container, discard the plastic wrap, and divide the dough into 4 equal parts. On a floured surface, roll each dough into a 1/4 to 1/3-inch thickness. The longer the dough is, the thicker the cookie will be.

6. Place 2 pieces about 1 inch apart on a well-greased baking sheet. Place the baking sheet in a 180-degree (for the best brownies) oven and bake for 15 to 20 minutes. Let the cookies cool and handle with care.

7. The cookies will be firm but still moist. Place a large handful of cookie dough in the center and fill the top with chopped nuts or dried fruits.

8. Enjoy!
