A 2014 novel by Elizabeth Siminoff tells the story of a young woman who finds happiness with her brother, who helps protect her during a war. Guenevere meets Oberon at first sight, but he refuses to trust her as he doesn't know who she thinks she is. Oberon decides to marry her after spending time reading about her, but some disapprove of the union. The story ends with the wedding of Guenevere and Oberon. The book suggests that an older man is rejecting his feelings by not doing anything with his sexuality while his younger brother tries to overcome his gender identity by marrying his sister at a young age. This is similar to the experience of Mabel vs Scarlax's comic book, where a female is called 'rejecting' her sexual identity. The book also suggests that it is fair use to describe a female as having her sexual identity rejected.
