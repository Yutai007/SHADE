Ingredients:

For the crust:
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- 2 to 3 tbsp ice water

For the filling:
- 1 cup fresh or frozen cranberries
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 1 tsp grated orange zest
- 1/4 tsp salt

For the crumb topping:
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cut into small pieces

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.

2. In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, and salt for the crust. Add cold butter and use pastry cutter or fingers to work it into mixture until resembles coarse crumbs. Gradually stir in ice water until dough comes together.

3. Press dough into 9-inch tart pan with removable bottom, ensuring covers evenly. Use fork to prick bottom of crust several times. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until edges are lightly golden.

4. While crust is baking, prepare filling. In medium saucepan, combine cranberries, sugar, orange juice, cornstarch, orange zest, and salt. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until mixture thickens and cranberries burst, about 10-12 minutes.

5. Pour cranberry mixture into partially baked crust and spread evenly.

6. For crumb topping, in a separate bowl, mix flour, rolled oats, and brown sugar. Add cold butter and use pastry cutter or fingers to work into mixture until crumbles form.

7. Sprinkle crumb topping evenly over cranberry filling.

8. Bake an additional 25-30 minutes, until top is browned.
