Plot Summary [edit]

One of the books on a shelf in Eowyn's room at The Two Towers inn, and the third in the trilogy.

In a small village near Yendor where they lived, there was an underground passage that led straight to the Wall itself - but only just barely, and it seemed unlikely that anyone would be willing to use it.

It passed close to Suroth, a village held by the First King's Men and the local folk; some claimed to hear singing below that only men could hear.

The Lightbringer was set down with these stones before Eomer departed on his way northward. That would make it less likely that he would wander over the mountains after the battle has begun.
