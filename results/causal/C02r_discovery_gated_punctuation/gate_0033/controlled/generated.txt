Darius Latham (born May 20, 1988) is an American football defensive tackle. He was drafted by the Indianapolis Colts in the sixth round of the 2014 NFL Draft. He played college football at Michigan. Prior to this, he attended St. Joseph's in Metro-Detroit and Southfield High School in Southfield, Michigan. He was a member of the American Football team. Latham has four siblings, Sharnell, Rashaad, Dazmon, and Danae.

## Early years[edit] 

Latham played with the Plymouth Whalers, a Canadian Hockey League Junior Division team, from 1997–1999. He then attended Michigan Technological University and Michigan High School in Michigan. He was a member of the American Football team. Latham has four siblings, Sharnell, Rashaad, Dazmon, and Danae.

## Professional career[edit] 

### Indianapolis Colts[edit] 

On December 21, 2014, Latham made two tackles in a 20–17 victory over the Houston Texans in Week 15. On November 15, 2015, Latham recorded his first career tackle on quarterback Ryan Fitzpatrick in a 22–17 win over the New York Jets in Week 9. On September 9, 2016, against the Detroit Lions, Latham recorded a tackle on receiver Anquan Boldin for a one-yard loss during the 39–35 Lions victory.
