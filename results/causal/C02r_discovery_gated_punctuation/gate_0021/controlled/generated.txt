Arie Lev Gruzman (Hebrew: ארי לעבד גרוזמן, born July 1, 1948) is an American academic and scholar of Jewish studies. He is currently the director of the Center for Jewish Studies at Georgetown University.

Gruzman was born in Tel Aviv, Israel, to a family of Holocaust survivors. He received his undergraduate degree from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem in 1969, and his Ph.D. in Near Eastern Languages and Civilizations from Harvard University in 1977.

After completing his doctorate, Gruzman held academic positions at the University of Pennsylvania and the University of California, Berkeley, before joining the faculty at Georgetown in 1985. At Georgetown, he has served as the director of the Center for Jewish Studies since 1995.

Gruzman's research interests include Jewish history, Jewish thought, and the relationship between Judaism and Christianity. He has published numerous articles and books on these topics, including "The Jews and the Dead Sea Scrolls" (1992) and "The Jewish Jesus: How Jewish Theology Influenced the New Testament" (2000).
