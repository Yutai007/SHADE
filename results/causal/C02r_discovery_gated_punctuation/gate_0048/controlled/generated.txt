Rare Ltd., a UK-based production company, announced today that a sequel to the beloved 2000 video game, Blinx, is in the works. The original game, a milestone in the casual gaming genre, sold over 2 million copies worldwide and was praised for its colorful graphics and fun gameplay. The sequel, set to release in 2024, will feature new gameplay mechanics, including a revamped upgrade system and improved controls, allowing players to fully immerse themselves in the world of Blinx. The game will be available on the Xbox Series X and S, PlayStation 5, and will support 4K resolution and high-frame rate gameplay. A release date has not been announced yet, but Rare Ltd. plans to release the game in 2024.
