Peking duck is a beloved dish with a long history in China. Originating in Beijing, it has gained popularity worldwide.

Ingredients:

- 1 whole duck (4 to 5 pounds)
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup maltose syrup (you can substitute with brown sugar)
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons dry sherry
- 1 tablespoon Chinese five-spice powder
- 1 tablespoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground star anise
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup sliced green onion
- 1/2 cup diced shallot
- 1/2 cup diced fresh pineapple

Instructions:

1. To make the glaze, combine the honey, maltose syrup, soy sauce, dry sherry, five-spice powder, red pepper flakes, ginger, garlic powder, cinnamon, and star anise in a small bowl. Set aside.

2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the duck and cook until browned, about 5 minutes per side.

3. Transfer the duck to a baking dish and brush with the glaze. Roast in the preheated oven for 30 minutes, brushing with the glaze every 10 minutes.

4. Let the duck rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

5. While the duck is roasting, heat a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the green onion, shallot, and pineapple and stir-fry until softened, about 5 minutes.

6. Serve the sliced duck with the stir-fried vegetables. Enjoy!
