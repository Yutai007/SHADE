In 1976, Dell Parsons, a 14-year-old, searches for his 16-year-old brother, Berner, who runs away from home. Dell tries to understand adolescence and catches up to Berner several times, but something always prevents him. He encounters a criminal named Richard Short and his gang, who are hiding in a rural motel; an elderly couple, the Novaks, who are in Idaho; and an ex-Marine, Earl Dee, who is feuding with his father. Dell discovers that his brother has been sexually involved with their mother and that his father knew all along but did nothing. When Dell is last seen, he is heading west, "looking for a sea that doesn't exist."
