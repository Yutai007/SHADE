Congratulations to all players of DAISAN NO BAKUDAN on surviving the "ZA DUST" update! This update introduces a new character, "Yuma," a new "Partner" system to strengthen your allies, and the new "ZA" difficulty level to really test your might.

Yuma is an agile character wielding a giant kitchen knife, as big as himself! His unique Partner ability doubles the damage output of any ally character for a limited time. Don't miss out on this technique in your battles!

The new ZA difficulty level is a true challenge. Enemies have more health and deal more damage, and they can use advanced techniques like counterattacks, dodging, and guarding. You'll have to bring your A-game to defeat these foes and emerge victorious.
