The people of Aquilonia gather in Zingara to witness King Conan sacrifice his lover, Valeria, by casting her into an insignificant abyss. As she rises like a phoenix, flying out of the pit, many watching fall away screaming. Valeria emerges from the depths and leaps onto a rock formation. It seems she survives. Subo, a dwarf, gives her food and water from his horn. She takes the food, and it rejuvenates her. The group decides to leave together, sensing the festival's origins and the evil forces it may contain. As they leave, they are hunted by a group of people led by Rexallon, who believes they are fleeing from King Conan. Both are captured. When Conan approaches, he tells Valeria it would be best if he kills her now to save everyone. Valeria asks him why he doesn't simply do his duty. King Ged intervenes, offering an alternative: both will journey out with Rexallon, accompanied by people under his command, to find the fabled Stone of Power—- the jeweled amulet and the fabled Shambhala—- the stone holds great power if it is put correctly around someone's neck.
