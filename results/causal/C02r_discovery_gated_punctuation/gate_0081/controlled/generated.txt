Delightful Lemon Cake Recipe

Ingredients:
-2 cups all-purpose flour
-1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
-¼ teaspoon salt
-3 large eggs, room temperature
-1 cup granulated sugar
-2 tablespoons lemon zest
-¾ cup fresh lemon juice
-1 cup buttermilk, room temperature
-½ cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled
-2 teaspoons vanilla extract

For the glaze:
-1 cup powdered sugar
-2-3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice

Instructions:

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and prepare a 9x13 inch baking pan by greasing it with cooking spray or butter and coating it with flour.

2. In a medium-sized bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.

3. In a separate large bowl, beat the eggs and sugar until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes using an electric mixer. Add in the lemon zest and juice and continue mixing until well incorporated.

4. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet mixture while alternating with the buttermilk, beginning and ending with the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.

5. Stir in the melted and cooled butter and vanilla extract until fully mixed.

6. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and smooth out the top using a spatula. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

7. Allow the cake to cool completely before making the glaze. To make the glaze, whisk together the powdered sugar and lemon juice in a small bowl until smooth. Drizzle over the cooled cake and let it set for several hours before serving. Enjoy!
