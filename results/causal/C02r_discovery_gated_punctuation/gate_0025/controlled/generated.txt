Ingredients:
For the dumplings:
One 16-ounce package of square dumpling wrappers or round wrappers
1 pound crabmeat
1/2 cup all-purpose flour
2 tablespoons minced green onion
2 tablespoons minced fresh ginger
1 tablespoon soy sauce
1 tablespoon sake or dry sherry
1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
1/2 cup thinly sliced green onion tops

For the dipping sauce:
1/4 cup soy sauce
1 tablespoon rice vinegar
1 tablespoon honey
1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, mix together the crabmeat, flour, minced green onion, minced ginger, soy sauce, sake or sherry, sesame oil, salt, and pepper.
2. Place 1 heaping spoonful of filling on each wrapper. (Save any leftover filling for another use.)
3. Dip your finger into water and moisten the edges of the wrapper. Fold opposite corners together, pressing to seal. Arrange filled dumplings on a baking sheet dusted with flour.
4. Prepare a basket steamer. Stack the dumplings, touching but not overlapping. Cover and steam for about 10 minutes, or until the wrappers are cooked and the filling is hot.
5. In a small bowl, mix all dipping sauce ingredients and whisk to blend.
6. Place the hot dumplings on a plate and sprinkle with green onion tops. Serve immediately with the dipping sauce.
