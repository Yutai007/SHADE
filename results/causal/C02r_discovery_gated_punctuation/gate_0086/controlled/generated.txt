Avi Gabbay, born on August 16, 1968, is an Israeli politician who served as the leader of the Labor Party from 2016 to 2019. He previously held the position of Knesset member for the party and was the Minister of Transportation and the Minister of Environmental Protection.

Gabbay was born in Tel Aviv and studied law at Tel Aviv University. He joined the Labor Party during his youth and first entered the Knesset in 2003. Over a decade, he rose through the ranks, becoming the secretary-general of the party from 2012 to 2016 before being elected as its leader in 2016.

While serving as transportation minister, Gabbay oversaw projects such as constructing Jerusalem's light rail system and expanding Tel Aviv's metro. He also promoted electric and hybrid vehicles and initiated a program to encourage bicycle usage.

As environmental protection minister, Gabbay implemented policies to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and promote renewable energy. He also worked to protect Israel's natural resources, such as its beaches and parks.

In the 2019 Israeli legislative election, the Labor Party, led by Gabbay, failed to win any seats in the Knesset. Gabbay resigned as party leader and returned to his private law practice.
