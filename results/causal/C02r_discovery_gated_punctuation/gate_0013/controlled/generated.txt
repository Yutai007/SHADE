In just 25 minutes, this 1937 Walt Disney classic is an animated masterpiece, the first classic fairytale produced by Walt Disney himself. Set in a time when there lived a beautiful young girl named Snow White. One day, a huntsman was sent to find a deer for his evil queen, but instead, he found Snow White hiding in the woods. When he brought her back to the castle, the queen asked him if he had killed her. He told her that she was still alive. One day, while Snow White was picking flowers, the Evil Queen appeared and cursed her to die at the age of 16. The Queen did not kill Snow White but took her away by the huntsman. The huntsman warned her about who she was trying to fool. Soon after, Snow White came to the dark forest where seven dwarfs met her and told her their story. However, one dwarf warned her against it. As they traveled to the dwarves' cottage, all the trees followed each dwarf's footsteps by dancing for their king. On the way, the dwarfs stopped next to a stream, and one wanted to see what the inside looked like. So he put his eye to a keyhole, hearing Snow White scream. The other dwarfs rushed outside to find out what happened, only to discover blood on one of the dwarves' beards and a bird flying with an apple in its beak.
