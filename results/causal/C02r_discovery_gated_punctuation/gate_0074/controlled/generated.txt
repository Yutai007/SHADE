This paper introduces CIDI-Lung-Seg, a single-click annotation tool for automatically delineating lungs from CT scans. The tool uses a pre-trained deep learning model to identify and segment the lungs in scans with just a single click. A blinded, controlled study comparing its performance to manual annotation found high agreement (96.2% overall) and a kappa statistic of 0.87. The tool also saves significant time, with an average time saving of 87.2%. This method has the potential to greatly reduce the time required for manually annotating lung cancer scans.
