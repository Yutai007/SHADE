Medical image segmentation is crucial for clinical analysis and computer-aided diagnosis. Unlike 2D images, 3D medical images offer more detailed structures and robust segmentation capabilities. However, effectively utilizing the rich information in 3D medical images remains a challenge. This paper introduces UXNet, a novel deep learning architecture for 3D medical image segmentation. UXNet employs a unique upsampled encoder-decoder network with a multi-level feature aggregation mechanism. This mechanism enhances the spatial information by upsampling low-level features and captures complex structures by aggregating multi-level features. UXNet learns more robust representations, improving segmentation accuracy. Extensive experiments on three benchmark datasets, including ISLES2017, LLC, and SYNAPSE-BREAST-CT, show UXNet's effectiveness and robustness compared to state-of-the-art methods. The source code and models are available at https://github.com/CCF-Union/UXNet.
